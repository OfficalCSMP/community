About the Event

CSMP Season 1 is a competitive Minecraft event focused on survival, teamwork, and creative strategy. Compete through seasonal objectives, server-wide challenges, and special event days.

How it works

  • Join as a solo player or team.
  • Complete weekly tasks and milestone goals.
  • Earn points through challenges and PvP events.

Prizes

  • Top teams receive community rewards and recognition.
  • Special badges and season winner titles.
  • Bonus rewards for standout builders and creators.

Important details

  • Season: CSMP Season 1
  • Server type: Competitive SMP event
  • Rules, schedule, and updates are posted in Discord.

About CSMP
