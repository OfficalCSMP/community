04 / Intel

Frequently asked
questions

What is CSMP?

CSMP is a competitive Minecraft event where 100 creators compete for the title of Champion.

Who can participate in CSMP?

CSMP is designed specifically for content creators who are invited or selected to participate.

When is CSMP Season 1?

The official date for Season 1 will be announced through our official channels.

How can I become a CSMP participant?

Participants are selected by @ItsMo. Creators are chosen based on their content, personality, and overall fit for CSMP. There is currently no application process.

Is CSMP free to participate in?

Yes. CSMP does not charge creators to participate.

Where can I find official announcements?

Only trust announcements posted through our official CSMP channels and website.

Is there an official CSMP Discord?

Yes. This is the official CSMP Discord community. Be careful of servers or accounts impersonating CSMP.

How can I contact the moderation team?

Email us at support@c-smp.net and wait patiently for a member of the CSMP Moderation Team to respond.

Where can I find official socials?

Official links are shared in the 🔗┃socials channel of this Discord. Only links provided there should be considered official.
